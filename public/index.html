⚙ How the Numbers Are Computed

The AI never sees a raw threshold like a normal PLC alarm. It compares every new reading against a learned baseline — what this sensor normally reads at the current load / temperature / speed — and scores how far the new reading deviates from that expected pattern.

1. Anomaly score (Isolation Forest concept)
deviation = |reading − baseline(load)| ÷ normal spread
→ higher deviation across recent samples = higher anomaly score
2. Remaining Useful Life (Weibull survival model)
RUL(t) = RUL(t−1) + trend_rate × Δt
→ the AI fits a slow degradation trend across days, not one bad reading
3. Decision rule
fault probability > 25% → maintenance advisory
RUL < 60 days → work order auto-generated
safety limit crossed → SIS trips independently (never the AI)
